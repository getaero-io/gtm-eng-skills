# BuyerCaddy internal trial

Available only to deepline.com and getaero.io staff with the Deepline-managed key.
Search products by search text or categoryId; list categories to obtain category IDs.
Use companyDomain for installed technology and competitor lookups, vendorDomain for vendor products.
The company products endpoint is paginated. File export is excluded from this lookup-only trial; export must be omitted or false.
Pricing is explicitly zero for this internal trial. Replace endpoint prices and the purchased-credit USD rate before customer release.
