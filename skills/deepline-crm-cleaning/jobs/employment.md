# Check employment

**Input:** person identity, role history, employer identity, evidence dates, and freshness policy.

1. Match evidence to the person.
2. Compare effective and observation dates with the accepted state.
3. Distinguish an employer move, promotion, concurrent job, advisory role, rebrand, and acquisition.
4. Resolve employer identity before selecting an account.
5. Propose a dated employment link.
6. Keep concurrent roles and unknown dates. A missing result does not prove a job ended.
7. Verify proposed work email and phone values.
8. Preserve suppression and history.
9. Propose current association changes.
10. Keep past deals and activities on the account that was correct at the time.

**Output:** dated role proposals, contact checks, unresolved cases, and `employment` receipts.

**Complete when:** late or repeated events cannot reverse newer accepted evidence. Report employer and title accuracy, false changes, freshness, and unresolved coverage. A job change does not authorize reassignment; apply the safety rule.
