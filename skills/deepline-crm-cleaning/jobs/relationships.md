# Check relationships

**Input:** identities, existing links, typed evidence, routing policy, and CRM relationship limits.

1. Inventory contact-account links, hierarchy, deals, activities, owners, and custom links.
2. Preserve native direction and labels.
3. Resolve identity before proposing links.
4. Use employment, account-unit, and hierarchy evidence. A shared domain cannot select an account.
5. Separate factual links from owner and routing choices.
6. Keep technical connections as evidence.
7. Show proposed additions and removals, dates, link counts, and downstream effects.
8. Retain types the CRM cannot represent in the neutral graph.
9. Send approved changes through the repair path.
10. Check primary links, permissions, and historical records after execution.

**Output:** link proposals, unresolved conflicts, preservation checks, and `relationships` receipts.

**Complete when:** each link has a supported type and direction or an unresolved reason. Keep current employment separate from historical deals. Apply the safety rule to every assignment.
