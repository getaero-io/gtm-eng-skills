# States and Plays

The reader should understand one distinction: a state records progress; a Play performs work under an explicit transition rule.

The diagram follows one candidate through research and fit assessment. It assigns business rules to the operator, implementation to the coding agent, version history to GitHub and execution to Deepline. Task-fit assessment and project research are labeled proposed extensions.

The SVG was rendered to a 4000 × 2250 PNG with the create-diagram renderer. The overlap report contains no collisions. The full-size output was visually inspected for clipping, hierarchy, arrow direction and readable labels.
