# Simple introduction

- Audience question: How does each step increase activated and retained experts?
- Headline: Maximize activated and retained experts.
- Supporting line: Measure useful contributions, not just names in a recruiting queue.
- Story beats: find experts; qualify and invite; create scenarios; learn and re-engage.
- Visual thesis: each stage contributes a measurable input to accepted work and returning contributors.
- Exclusions: provider names except the outreach destination, database tables, code, costs, detailed state transitions and individual candidate information.

The four-step flow is the product story, not the execution order of one recruiting invocation. Blue shows real recruiting capabilities; neutral shows the simulated creation/learning portion. The dashed handoff marks that the live recruiting queue is not automatically connected to the synthetic lifecycle. The footer distinguishes the callable below-100 rule from an active recurring trigger and from sending messages.

The November 22 metric revision adds one leading measure per visual stage and the RCE28 outcome. The detailed scorecard supplies denominators, maturity windows, owners and intervention signals. Outcome definitions are proposed and downstream production measurement is not yet instrumented.
