# Host Dossier One-Pager Outline

## Template structure

### Header
- **Photo** (cached from LinkedIn or placeholder)
- **Name** (large, bold)
- **Title at Company**
- **LinkedIn URL** (small, bottom of header)

### Section 1: Why this person matters
2-3 sentences:
- ICP fit / TAL status
- Seniority / influence
- Relevance to event topic or host's goals

### Section 2: Background (3-5 bullets)
- Current role (brief description)
- Prior roles (1-2 most relevant)
- Education (if prestigious or relevant)
- Notable projects or achievements (public only)

**Source:** LinkedIn profile

### Section 3: Research highlights (3-8 bullets)
From recent public sources:
- Recent company news (funding, product launch, expansion)
- Blog posts or talks
- Shared connections or communities
- Relevant interests or side projects

**Sources:** Company website, LinkedIn posts, Crunchbase, tech blogs, conference sites

### Section 4: Talk tracks
**Warm intro score:** X/10

**Name-game hooks:**
- Shared connection: {{name}}
- Prior employer overlap: {{company}}
- Conference or community: {{event}}

**Topics to raise:**
- {{topic_1}}
- {{topic_2}}
- {{topic_3}}

**Avoid:**
- {{sensitive_topic_if_any}}

### Footer
- Segment: `hot` or `warm`
- Generated: {{date}}
- Do not share externally without permission
