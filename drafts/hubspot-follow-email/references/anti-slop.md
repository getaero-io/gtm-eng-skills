# Anti-Slop Rules for HubSpot Emails

## Banned phrases

Never use these in GTM emails:

- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "revolutionary"
- "unlock" (verb)
- "leverage" (verb)
- "crush" / "crushing it"
- "ninja" / "rockstar" / "guru"
- "synergy"
- "circle back"
- "touch base"
- "low-hanging fruit"
- "move the needle"
- "take it to the next level"
- "drinking from the firehose"
- "boil the ocean"

## Passive-aggressive patterns

❌ "Just following up on my previous email"  
✅ "Quick note about {{specific_thing}}"

❌ "Wanted to make sure this didn't get lost"  
✅ "Following up on {{specific_offer}}"

❌ "Circling back per my last email"  
✅ Omit entirely or restate clearly

## Vague claims

❌ "We help companies like yours grow faster"  
✅ "We help {{segment}} reduce {{specific_cost}} by {{specific_method}}"

❌ "Our platform empowers teams"  
✅ "Our {{tool_name}} does {{specific_job}}"

❌ "Join 1000+ companies"  
✅ Either name 3 real logos (with permission) or skip social proof entirely

## CTA traps

❌ Three competing CTAs: "Book a demo" + "Download whitepaper" + "Join webinar"  
✅ One primary CTA; one optional secondary at most

❌ "Schedule a quick call" (what does "quick" mean?)  
✅ "Book a 15-minute walkthrough"

## Subject lines

❌ "Quick question"  
❌ "Following up"  
❌ "Checking in"  
✅ {{event_name}} in {{city}} — {{date}}  
✅ {{resource_name}} is ready

## Personalization

❌ "I saw you're at {{company}} and thought this would be relevant"  
✅ "You're at {{company}} — this is for {{specific_role_or_challenge}}"

❌ Fake personalization that applies to everyone  
✅ Real conditional content or no personalization at all

## When in doubt

- Remove adjectives (especially superlatives)
- Remove adverbs
- State the fact, the offer, and the next step
- Read it aloud — if it sounds like a LinkedIn influence post, rewrite
