# Brand Voice Guidelines

## Voice principles

- **Systems over tactics.** Frame work as repeatable plays, not one-off hacks.
- **Honest tradeoffs.** Acknowledge cost, time, and reliability gaps — don't oversell.
- **No hype words.** Avoid: game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, leverage (verb), ninja, rockstar, crushing it.

## Tone

- Conversational but precise
- Collaborative, not salesy
- Show respect for the reader's time and intelligence

## What good looks like

✅ "We're hosting a GTM meetup in SF — limited to 40 seats. RSVP here."  
❌ "Join us for a game-changing networking experience that will revolutionize your pipeline!"

✅ "Pilot with 10 rows before committing to a full enrich run."  
❌ "Unlock unlimited enrichment and crush your quota!"

## Invites specifically

- One primary CTA
- State capacity limit explicitly
- Use speaker credentials (title + company), not hype adjectives
- Keep subject line ≤50 characters
- No pressure language ("don't miss out," "last chance," "limited time")
